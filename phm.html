The desk

Your office is an operating system. GM Office, My Team, Ledger, the Player Market, News, and the Archives each open in their own window. There is an air hockey game hiding on the desktop. Nobody will tell you where.

The rink

Live games play out event by event on a rink you can read. The heatmap under the play is the distribution the engine is actually sampling from, and your tactical calls bend it while the game runs. Faceoffs land on the dots. Wraparounds go in at the rate they really go in.

The league

The Premier Hockey League: 32 clubs with histories, awards, and a cup worth wanting. Every player identity is measured from five seasons of real NHL play-by-play, then lives its own life in your franchise. Roster and schedule import packs are launch features, so the league can become whoever you want it to be.
